CHOOSE THE BEST SETTING FOR OUR WORK
I offer online and in-person consultations or a combination of both.

Online

For the deepest comfort

I offer online consultations or a combination of online and in-person meetings. The convenience of virtual sessions means I can reach my clients all over the world. You can be at the comfort of your own home or anywhere you are able to create a little space where you are not going to be disturbed. You will need an internet connection and access to Zoom account. Let me know if you need some assistance with Zoom setup. Once our session is booked, I will email you a link and by clicking on it, you will get to a secure virtual room where we meet.

In-Person

For the deepest embodiment

I offer in-person consultations in a cosy and spacious therapy room in the City – Liverpool Street London. The space is part of Integrity Centre – an urban oasis, where you can disconnect from the daily grind and reconnect with your authentic self through psychotherapy, yoga, pilates,  and other therapies.  The office is easily accessible by public transport. It is located by Liverpool Street station (1 minute walk). In-person sessions can be alternated with online sessions. We can find the most suitable arrangement for you and your life.

In-Nature

For the deepest grounding

How would you feel about meeting me in nature, away from the hustle and bustle of the busy city life, and unconstrained by the four walls of your room and the four corners of your computer screen? Being surrounded by nature offers an immersive experience that can foster a greater connection to your sense of self and to the natural world we are an integral part of. My favourite place to meet is Fairlop Waters Country Park, it is a magical place where in a peaceful and quiet space we can walk, sit by the water and explore our experiences of how the outer world meets your inner reality.

CANCELLATION POLICY
I work with a 7-day cancellation policy. Cancellations made with 7 days’ notice will not be chargeable, otherwise the full fee in payable. The payments should be made to the bank account prior to our meeting. I provide receipts upon request.

CONFIDENTIALITY
Please rest assured that your inquiry, as well as our sessions, will be treated in confidence. Confidentiality is at the heart of building a safe and trusting space to explore sensitive and intimate issues. This is what makes the therapy relationship different to other relationships. The content of our conversations will remain confidential during your therapy and thereafter. There are some limitations to confidentiality and I will discuss them with you in our first meeting.

I am bound to the Ethical Framework for Good Practice in Counselling & Psychotherapy as elaborated by the UKCP (United Kingdom Council for Psychotherapy).

My practice is based in the UK. By making use of my therapy service you agree that the service is governed by English law and that the English judiciary has exclusive jurisdiction.
